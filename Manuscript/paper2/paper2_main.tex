\section{Gauge Transformation of the Nonlocal Pseudopotential Term}
\label{app:gauge_nlpp}

\myblue{[Full derivation of the gauge transformation of V\^{}nl from
length to velocity gauge, following the structure of Mattiat \& Luber
(2022) Appendix B but adapted to the Kleinman-Bylander form used in
RMG. This is the extended algebra supporting section~\ref{sec:theory}
subsection on current operator and nonlocal pseudopotentials.]}

\section{Equivalence of Current-Based and Berry Phase Polarization}
\label{app:berry}

\myblue{[Proof that the velocity gauge current J(t), when integrated,
gives the same polarization as the King-Smith/Vanderbilt Berry phase
formula. This supports the comparison presented in
section~\ref{sec:benchmarks}.]}
